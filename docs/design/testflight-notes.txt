Night Watch

Telos has a new charcoal-and-brass design, custom typography, and a clearer camp.
Planning, squad equipment, crafting, and upgrades now show the important choices
first, with details available on tap.

Also new: an updated field manual, readable expedition history, guild name and
sigil editing, clearer backup reviews, and redesigned expedition share cards.
The widget and lock-screen activity now match Night Watch.

Your existing guild should carry forward. Expedition balance, sector time
requirements, and Integrity rules remain the same.

What to test:
- Complete a normal 25-minute session with the phone locked. Check the return
  notification, Integrity, report, and rewards.
- Try a longer session if it fits your routine. Reopen once mid-run and confirm
  the timer stays correct.
- Compare and swap equipment, craft or upgrade, then restart the app.
- Export a backup and share an expedition card. The image should contain no task
  or reflection text.
- Report clipped text, confusing navigation, or lost/duplicated progress with
  your iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to it.
